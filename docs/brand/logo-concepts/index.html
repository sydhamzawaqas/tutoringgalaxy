Tutoring Galaxy: logo concepts

Ten new logo directions drawn from scratch to replace the AI-generated 3D medallion. Each mark is built on a 64×64 grid from simple geometry, uses at most two colours, and is shown in light, dark and one-colour versions, at real sizes (down to a 16px favicon) and as a full lockup with the name.

How a good logo works

SimpleA few shapes you could sketch from memory after one look. No bevels, glows or 3D lighting.
Works smallMust still read as a 16px favicon, a WhatsApp profile picture and an app icon.
One colour firstMust work as a single flat colour, for print, stamps, embroidery and photo overlays. Colour is a bonus.
Carries one ideaOne clear story (guidance, progress, a mentor relationship), not every symbol at once.
OwnableDistinct from competitors and from stock icons, so people remember it as yours.
A system, not a pictureIcon, lockup with the name, favicon, social avatar, and light/dark versions, all from the same shapes.

Why the old logo has to go

Old medallion
  • 3D gradients and lighting: turns into a gold blob at 16–32px
  • Can't be reproduced in one colour
  • A badge with ten orbiting objects: no single idea
  • Reads as a generic award or military badge, not tutoring
New concepts
  • Flat vectors, two colours, built on a grid
  • Tested at 16/32/64px on light and dark backgrounds
  • Each concept tells one story
  • Name lockup included, ready for the site header

The 10 concepts

Scores are a design judgement (5 = best). Recommended: 07 Spiral Galaxy (most distinctive, and it doubles as a "G"), 02 Orbit G (clearest at small sizes), and 08 Constellation of Progress (best story for parents).

Next steps

  1. Pick 2–3 favourites, or mix them (for example the Spiral Galaxy with the constellation's progress idea).
  2. Refine the chosen mark: optical balance, stroke weights per size (a simplified favicon cut), and spacing rules.
  3. Lock the colours and type with the palette and Google Font chosen in the inspiration round. The lockup uses Plus Jakarta Sans as a placeholder.
  4. Deliver the kit: outlined-text lockups, favicon.ico and PNG set, app icons, OG image, WhatsApp/social avatars, and a one-page usage guide.

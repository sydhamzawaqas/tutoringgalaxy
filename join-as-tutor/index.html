Teach with Tutoring Galaxy

We match tutors with students on the exact syllabus they've taught, from O Level and IGCSE to IB, MDCAT and SAT. Lessons are one-to-one, online or at home.

Questions before you apply? WhatsApp us or email tutoringgalaxy@gmail.com.

Who we look for

  • You have taught a specific syllabus before, and know its papers and mark schemes
  • You can explain an idea simply, then check that the student really understood it
  • You are reliable: on time, prepared and in touch with parents
  • Usually, a degree in your subject or a closely related one
  • A laptop, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to teach online

How it works

  1. 1

    Apply

    Tell us what you teach, which exam boards and levels, and your experience.

    [10 minutes]
  2. 2

    Short conversation

    If your experience matches what families are asking for, we'll call or message you to talk about how you teach.

  3. 3

    Demo lesson

    You teach a short sample lesson on a topic from a syllabus you know, so we can see how you explain.

  4. 4

    Start teaching

    We match you with students on the syllabuses and levels you've taught, online or near you.
